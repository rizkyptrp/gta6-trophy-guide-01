Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies and no mission replay — so save by hand. There are 15 manual save slots plus one autosave: keep one at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission. The order below is built from the official descriptions: story first, side strands next, then robbery and partner trophies, then the long collectible and money grinds, with 100% completion last.

    ⚠ The one missable trophy

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida. One spot, Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1, disappears when the chapter ends. After Lucia is home, check the kitchen in the morning (a morning after the mission Down to Earth is the usual time): she heads to the balcony of Jason's house to meditate. Follow her and accept the optional task. Keep a Chapter 1 save until it pops. Sources: GTABase and PowerPyx (missable flag), Output Lag (the balcony scene).

    Also time-sensitive, not missable: the Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 carries an hourglass icon and its strand counts toward Strangers & Freaks — race the moment it appears. Source: Output Lag.
